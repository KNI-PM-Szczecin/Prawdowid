# Landing (src/frontend/src/components/Landing.tsx)

Mode: Persuade (visitor pastes a text and trusts the tool enough to act on the result). Audience: citizen with doubts about a message; secondary: jury auditing claims. Must stay: form as the first action, demo examples in mock mode, accessibility panel, Prompt font, Figma world. Would feel wrong: marketing hype, invented numbers, anything resembling a government portal's identity.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a public notice board of how a check is done, not a product pitch. It refuses the hero-plus-three-icon-cards scaffold and proves the mechanism by showing the real verdict vocabulary and source types the tool uses.

OWN-WORLD: Figma world extended: white ground, soft blurred blobs in verdict colours (green on home), Prompt in medium weights, black primary action, 2px outlined chips, verdict icons in rings. Formal register, generous measure, numbered process set as a document, not cards.

STORY: Visitor understands what happens after pressing „Sprawdź” (claims, catalog search, verdict with evidence), learns the five verdicts and that abstention is honest, sees the three source types, and learns the rules the tool refuses to break; then pastes a text.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Header (brand + accessibility). Centered display title, one-sentence lead, the textarea and black „Sprawdź” button, note on sources. Green blobs top-right and bottom-left behind.

FORM: extension of the established surface; seed: none (unavailable).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
